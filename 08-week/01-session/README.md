\# Week 08 - Session 01



\## Agile and DevOps for Distributed Teams



This session focused on Scrum, DevOps and flow management for teams working across multiple services and repositories.



\### Scrum workflow



Product Backlog → Sprint Backlog → Sprint → Increment → Review / Retrospective



\### Main concepts



\- Prioritized Product Backlog.

\- Sprint Backlog and Sprint Goal.

\- User stories with testable acceptance criteria.

\- Planning, daily sync, review and retrospective.

\- DevOps: the team builds and operates its services.

\- CI/CD and continuous feedback.

\- WIP limits to reduce unfinished work.

\- Lead time, cycle time and throughput.

\- Clear ownership for services and repositories.

\- Frequent pull requests and short-lived branches.



\### Application to OptiView



OptiView is distributed across multiple repositories and services. The team therefore requires clear ownership, small increments and continuous integration.



Work should flow from prioritized documentation and backlog items toward testable development increments. WIP limits help the team finish work before starting additional stories.



\## Key takeaway



A distributed architecture requires a coordinated team with clear responsibilities, testable stories, small increments and rapid feedback.
